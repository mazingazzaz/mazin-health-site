Mazin Health

Mazin Health is a private, personal iPhone app. It shows its owner's own health and training data in one place: body measurements, daily vitals, sleep, steps and workouts.

The app is not offered to the public. It is installed only on its owner's own devices.

Google Health (Fitbit) data

With the owner's permission, the app reads heart rate variability, resting heart rate, sleep, steps and activity from Google Health. Access is read-only. The app cannot change or delete anything in Google Health.
