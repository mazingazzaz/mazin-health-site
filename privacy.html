Privacy policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

Mazin Health is a personal app used only by its owner. This policy explains what it does with data.

What the app reads

When the owner connects a Google account, the app reads the following from Google Health (Fitbit), read-only: heart rate variability, resting heart rate, VO2 max, sleep, steps and activity.

How the data is used

The data is used only to show the owner their own health summary inside the app. It is not used for advertising, is not sold, and is not used to train AI or machine-learning models.

Where the data is stored

Daily summaries are stored on the owner's iPhone and in the owner's own private storage, which only the owner controls. The Google sign-in is kept in the iPhone's Keychain. The developer operates no server that receives Google Health data.

Sharing

Google Health data is not shared with or transferred to anyone.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access and data

The owner can disconnect at any time in the app's Settings, or revoke access at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the app removes the data stored on the phone.

Contact

Questions can be raised through the issues page of this site's repository.
